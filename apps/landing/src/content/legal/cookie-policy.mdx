## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. Cookies allow a site to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you visit.

## Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Service to function. They enable core features such as authentication, security, and session management. You cannot opt out of essential cookies, as the Service would not work without them.

## Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Service so we can improve it. They collect aggregated, anonymous information such as which pages are most visited and how users move through the site.

## Preference and optional cookies

These cookies remember choices you make, such as your preferred theme (light or dark) or language. They are optional — the Service works without them, but they improve your experience.

## Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect how the Service functions. Most browsers allow you to:

- View the cookies that are set.
- Block all or certain cookies.
- Delete cookies when you close your browser.
